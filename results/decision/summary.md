# Decision analysis of OOF predictions

Prioritisation and decision curves are re-weighted to a sinkhole prevalence of 0.091 (1:10); reliability, Brier and ECE are on the 1:1 benchmark sample. `found_per_100_inspected@10%` = expected sinkholes among 100 inspected sites when the top 10 % of sites are visited. `nb_beats_inspect_all_from_pt` = smallest threshold probability from which the model's net benefit exceeds inspecting every site (NaN = never within 0.05–0.8; probabilities prior-shifted to the 1:10 prevalence before thresholding). `platt_*` = after cross-fitted Platt scaling. The FGS favourability map is an ordinal class map (no probabilities), shown for ranking only. Tied scores (FGS classes, random-forest vote shares) enter the ranking curves and ECE as the expected value over random orders of the tied sites, so the numbers do not depend on how the sort breaks ties.
